# Baker's Crumbs — About Us, Location and Opening Hours

Baker's Crumbs is a small neighbourhood bakery (fictional business, invented for a class project).
We bake bread, pastries, cookies and cakes fresh every morning.

## Location
- Address: 12 Harina Street, Barangay Dulce, Mabuhay City (fictional)
- Landmark: across the street from Mabuhay Public Library
- Parking: 4 street parking slots in front of the shop; no private parking lot

## Opening hours
| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday to Friday | 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM |
| Saturday | 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM |

- Fresh bread comes out of the oven at 7:00 AM and 2:00 PM (6:00 AM on Saturdays).
- Holiday hours: closed on 25 December and 1 January. Other holidays follow Sunday hours.
