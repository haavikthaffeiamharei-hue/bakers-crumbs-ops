# Baker's Crumbs — Store Policies

## Cancellations and changes
- Pre-orders can be changed or cancelled up to 24 hours before pickup at no charge.
- Whole-cake down payments are refundable only if cancelled at least 48 hours before pickup.
- Custom cake down payments are non-refundable once decorating has started (2 days before pickup).

## Refunds and complaints
- If a product is damaged or incorrect, report it within 24 hours with a photo and the receipt.
- The bakery will replace the product or refund the price.
- Taste preferences are not grounds for a refund.

## Freshness and storage
- Breads and pastries are best eaten on the day of purchase.
- Pandesal keeps for 2 days in an airtight container at room temperature.
- Cakes with cream or cheese must be refrigerated and eaten within 3 days.
- Cookies keep for 5 days in an airtight container.

## Loyalty card
- One stamp for every ₱200 spent in one receipt.
- 10 stamps = one free Cookie Box of 12.
- Stamps are not given for delivery fees.

## Things the assistant cannot help with
- Placing, paying for, or tracking orders.
- Checking today's stock.
- Job applications, supplier enquiries or media requests (email hello@bakerscrumbs.example).
- Medical, allergy or dietary advice beyond the information in these documents.
