# Baker's Crumbs — Ingredients and Allergen Information

IMPORTANT: This information is general. Recipes and suppliers can change, and all products are made
in one shared kitchen. Customers with allergies must confirm with staff before buying or eating anything.
The assistant must never say a product is "safe" for someone with an allergy.

## Allergens present in our kitchen
Wheat (gluten), eggs, milk, butter, soy, peanuts, tree nuts (almonds, walnuts, cashews), sesame and coconut.
Cross-contact is possible for every product.

## Allergens by product group
| Product group | Contains |
|---|---|
| All breads and pandesal | Wheat (gluten). Most also contain milk and eggs. |
| Cookies | Wheat, eggs, milk, butter. Chocolate items contain soy. |
| Matcha White Chocolate Cookie | Wheat, eggs, milk, butter, soy. |
| Croissants and cinnamon rolls | Wheat, eggs, milk, butter. |
| Buko Pie | Wheat, milk, coconut. |
| Egg Pie | Wheat, eggs, milk. |
| Cakes | Wheat, eggs, milk. Mango Cream Cake may contain cashew crumble. |
| Classic Cheesecake | Milk, eggs, wheat (biscuit base). |

## Dietary options
- No products are gluten-free.
- No products are vegan.
- Whole Wheat Loaf has no eggs, but contains milk.
- Sugar-free products are not offered.

## Ingredients we use
- Butter (not margarine) in croissants, cookies and ensaymada.
- Ube halaya made in-house.
- Fresh carabao mango when in season; mango purée when out of season.
