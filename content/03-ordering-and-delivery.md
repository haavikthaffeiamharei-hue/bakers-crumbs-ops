# Baker's Crumbs — Ordering, Pickup and Delivery

The assistant cannot take orders or payments. It can only explain how ordering works.

## Walk-in
- All menu items can be bought at the counter during opening hours, while stocks last.

## Pre-orders (for pickup)
- Place pre-orders by phone or email at least 1 day ahead.
- Whole cakes and Cookie Boxes of 12 need at least 2 days' notice.
- Pre-orders are ready for pickup from 10:00 AM on the chosen day.
- A 50% down payment is required for whole cakes; other pre-orders are paid at pickup.

## Custom cakes
- Custom designs (names, colours, simple toppers) need at least 5 days' notice.
- Custom cakes start at ₱1,300 for an 8-inch round. The final price depends on the design.
- Tiered cakes and fondant designs are not offered.

## Delivery
- Delivery is available Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- Delivery area: within 5 km of the shop only (Barangay Dulce and neighbouring barangays in Mabuhay City).
- Delivery fee: ₱60 within 3 km, ₱90 for 3–5 km.
- Minimum order for delivery: ₱500.
- Orders outside the 5 km area cannot be delivered; customers may pick up instead.

## Payment methods
- Cash, GCash, Maya and major debit/credit cards at the counter.
- Pre-order down payments: GCash or Maya only.
